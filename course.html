Course outline

CSE 308 Design Project I — what the course covers and how it is organised, with every lab linked to its slide deck.

General information

CourseCSE 308 · Design Project I · project and laboratory course
MaterialsThe department’s Design Project I Manual (Labs I–X), rebuilt here as interactive decks
WorkTeams choose a software project in Lab I and grow it lab by lab: proposal, LaTeX report, SRS, SDLC model, DFDs, UML diagrams and UI wireframes
Final outputA team report and a poster presentation with viva

What you will be able to do

  • Form a team, choose a project and write a proposal with a feasibility study, a working plan, a Gantt chart and a budget.
  • Write technical reports in LaTeX.
  • Specify a system with an IEEE-style SRS and choose a suitable SDLC model.
  • Model the system with data flow diagrams and UML use case, sequence, communication and class diagrams.
  • Design the user interface with usability principles and Figma wireframes.

LabTopicSlides
Lab ITeam Formation, Project Assignment & Planning

Labs II–IIITechnical Report Writing with LaTeX
Typesetting reports in LaTeX with Overleaf: text and special characters, errors, mathematics, lists, packages, sections, cross-references, figures, tables, algorithms and BibTeX citations.		Open
Lab IV · open-endedIEEE Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
Functional and non-functional requirements, what makes a requirement good, how requirements are gathered, and how to write an SRS with the IEEE template, priorities and traceability.		Open
Lab VSDLC Model Selection
The six stages of the software development life cycle, the waterfall, iterative, V, spiral, agile and prototyping models, and how to choose one with a weighted comparison matrix.		Open
Lab VIData Flow Diagrams (DFD): Level 0 and Level 1
What a DFD shows, the four symbols in Gane–Sarson and Yourdon notation, the rules of valid data flows, and how to draw and balance Level 0 (context) and Level 1 diagrams, using the ATM system.		Open
Lab VIIUML Use Case Diagrams
Actors, use cases and system boundaries; association, include, extend and generalization; use case descriptions with main and alternative scenarios, using the ATM system.		Open
Lab VIIIUML Sequence & Communication Diagrams
Lifelines, activations and the eight message types; guards and combined fragments (alt, opt, loop); turning a use case into a sequence diagram; and the equivalent communication diagram.		Open
Labs IX–XUML Class Diagrams
Classes, attributes, operations and visibility; association, multiplicity, aggregation, composition and dependency; generalization, abstract classes, interfaces and realization, with the telephone and ATM examples.		Open
Lab: UI/UXUI/UX Foundations & Figma Wireframing
Usability, user-centred design, Nielsen’s heuristics, visual design and the laws of UX, accessibility with WCAG, fidelity levels, and designing and prototyping wireframes in Figma from your use cases.		Open
All labsWorked Project: CampusCare Appointment System
One realistic project taken through every step of the course: proposal and plan, LaTeX report, IEEE SRS, SDLC choice, DFD Level 0 and 1, use cases, sequence and communication diagrams, class diagram and Figma wireframes.		Open

Each lab exercise adds one chapter to the team’s report; see Lab reports for the checklists.
