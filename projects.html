Lab reports and the team project

Each lab exercise adds one chapter to your team’s report about your own system. Use the checklists before you submit; your ticks are saved in this browser.

Academic integrity. Copying from the internet, classmates or seniors is prohibited and can cost full marks. The checklists tell you what a good submission contains; the content must be your team’s own.

1. Proposal and planning

Lab I · topic 1: Team Formation, Project Assignment & Planning

Think about a new system with your team and submit a proposal covering the lab criteria.

2. Report in LaTeX

Labs II–III · topic 2: Technical Report Writing with LaTeX

Set up the team’s report on Overleaf using the template below.

3. SRS (open-ended)

Lab IV · topic 3: IEEE Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

Functional and non-functional requirements of your proposed system in the IEEE structure.

4. SDLC model selection

Lab V · topic 4: SDLC Model Selection

Select the best model for your system with a comparison matrix.

5. Data flow diagrams

Lab VI · topic 5: Data Flow Diagrams (DFD): Level 0 and Level 1

Level 0 and Level 1 DFDs (Level 2 optional) for your system.

6. Use case diagram

Lab VII · topic 6: UML Use Case Diagrams

Use case diagram and descriptions for your system.

7. Sequence and communication diagrams

Lab VIII · topic 7: UML Sequence & Communication Diagrams

For at least two use cases.

8. Class diagram

Labs IX–X · topic 8: UML Class Diagrams

Full class diagram of your system.

9. UI/UX wireframes

UI/UX lab · topic 9: UI/UX Foundations & Figma Wireframing

Wireframes and a clickable prototype in Figma.

LaTeX report template

A starting point for your team’s report with one chapter per lab. Upload it to Overleaf (New project → Upload), add references.bib and a figures/ folder.
